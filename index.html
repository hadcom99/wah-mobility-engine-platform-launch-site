Platform Capabilities

Built for every driver, operator, and dirham

The Wah platform closes every gap between earning intent and earning reality.

A single WahID verification activates ride-hail, food delivery, grocery, parcels, freight, and express courier from one app. Partners report a 38% increase in monthly active earning days within their first 60 days.

Eliminate commission opacity permanently

WahPay calculates cash-debt netting in real time — every fare, fee, and deduction is displayed before payout confirmation. No negative balance surprises, no end-of-day arithmetic.

Launch a new vertical in 90 days

SME logistics brands configure on top of Wah’s shared dispatch, dynamic pricing, and live tracking APIs instead of rebuilding them. First operators went live in 74 days from contract signature.

Operate precisely on 3G offline-first

How It Works

Live and earning in three steps

From zero to your first completed job — under 90 minutes on any 3G connection.

1

Verify once with WahID

Submit your national ID and a single biometric check — the process takes under 7 minutes on a 3G connection. Your verified WahID instantly activates all income verticals available in your wilaya.

2

Accept jobs across verticals

The Wah app surfaces ride-hail, food, parcel, and freight jobs in a single queue — no switching apps, no duplicate logins. WahDispatch routes the highest-yield job to your screen automatically.

3

Receive precise WahPay payouts

WahPay nets every fare, deduction, and cash advance in real time before you confirm receipt — your exact balance is visible before payout, not reconstructed afterwards.
